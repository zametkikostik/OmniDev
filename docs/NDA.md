## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship regarding the OmniDev software platform (the "Purpose"). The Owner may disclose confidential technical and commercial information.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by the Owner, including without limitation:

- source code, architecture, prompts, and internal tools;
- business plans, pricing, customer data, and metrics;
- API keys, infrastructure details, and security measures;
- any materials marked confidential or that a reasonable person would treat as confidential.

Public repository materials under the Proprietary LICENSE remain owned by the Owner; this NDA covers deeper access (private repos, staging, customer data, unreleased features).

## 3. Obligations

The Receiving Party shall:

- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent;
- protect it with at least the same care as its own confidential information (and no less than reasonable care);
- limit access to employees/contractors with a need to know who are bound by similar obligations.

## 4. Exclusions

Obligations do not apply to information that:

- is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party;
- was already known without confidentiality duty;
- is independently developed without use of Confidential Information;
- is required to be disclosed by law (with prior notice to the Owner where legally permitted).

## 5. Term

This NDA remains in effect for **3 (three) years** from the date of disclosure of the relevant information, or until the parties sign a superseding agreement.

## 6. Return / Destruction

Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing, except for copies retained under legal hold or automated backups (which remain confidential).

## 7. No License

Nothing in this NDA grants any license to OmniDev software, trademarks, or IP. Use of the Software requires a separate written license (see Commercial License Agreement).

## 8. Remedies

Unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm. The Owner is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

## 9. Governing Law

Governed by the laws of the Owner’s place of residence, without conflict-of-law rules.
